Appraisal uses

What the appraisal is for

Twelve private-party purposes, each with its own deadlines, effective dates and readers. Pick the one that matches the file.

Date of DeathA retrospective market value as of the date an owner died, prepared to support trust administration, estate tax returns and the step-up in basis.Estate & TrustIndependent market value reports for trustees, executors and estate attorneys handling living trusts, probate files, asset division and fiduciary accountings across San Diego County.Divorce AppraisalsNeutral market value reports for spouses, family law attorneys and mediators dividing a home or investment property in a San Diego County divorce.Bankruptcy ValuationsPetition-date market value reports supporting Schedule A/B, homestead exemption analysis and trustee negotiations in Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 cases.Prop 19 & TaxMarket value reports for Prop 19 parent-child transfers, assessment appeals and base year questions brought before the San Diego County Assessor.Family TransfersDocumented market value for gifts, intrafamily sales and sibling buyouts, supporting gift tax returns, fair pricing and property tax filings.Pre-PurchaseAn independent value check before an offer is written or finalized, showing a buyer how the asking price compares with real sales nearby.Pre-ListingAn unbiased market value before listing, helping sellers set a realistic price, compare agent opinions and decide which repairs are worth making.Insurance ClaimsMarket value documentation for owners, public adjusters and attorneys after fire, water, slope failure or other damage, including before-and-after value analysis.Bail BondsMarket value reports that show the equity available when real estate secures a court property bond or a bail agent's bond.Expert WitnessAppraisal reports, review work and testimony on residential value for attorneys handling estate, divorce, partition, bankruptcy and property damage disputes.I-864 SponsorsA recent appraisal by a licensed appraiser documenting home value so net equity can be counted on an Affidavit of Support filed with USCIS.

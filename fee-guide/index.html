Appraisal fees and report formats

Starting fees for each format, where visits are offered, and what moves a quote.

Pricing on this site is fixed by report type and published before any order is placed. The fee does not rise with the value of the home, and there is no surcharge for an Encinitas address versus an inland one. Some assignments do require more work than a typical tract home, and the sections below explain the circumstances that can add time or cost so there are no surprises once the report is underway.

What can raise a quote

Rush Delivery

Standard turnaround is several business days from the order. When a court hearing, a filing deadline or a closing date forces a faster schedule, a rush can often be arranged by moving other work. The added cost reflects that rescheduling, and the deadline is confirmed in writing before the order is accepted so the client knows exactly when the signed report will arrive.

Large Or Unusual Parcels

Olivenhain horse properties, multi-acre lots in Rancho Santa Fe and backcountry parcels with wells and septic systems often have few direct comparables. Extending the search over a wider area and a longer time frame, and supporting adjustments for acreage, outbuildings and water rights, takes more research than a typical subdivision home, and the quote reflects that before work begins.

Multiple Effective Dates

Certain divorces and estates require one property to be valued at two dates, for example a separation date and a trial date, or a date of death and today. Each additional date requires its own comparable search and market analysis. A combined order costs less than separate reports, and the quote lists each date so the scope is clear.

Litigation Support

Reports prepared for a contested matter may lead to depositions, declarations, rebuttal reviews of another appraiser's work or courtroom testimony. Because its extent varies with each case, that time is invoiced apart from the report. Attorneys can request an estimate for expected testimony time when ordering, and nothing beyond the written report is billed without prior agreement.

Complex Improvements

Homes with significant unpermitted additions, accessory dwelling units, mixed residential and agricultural use or major deferred maintenance require extra analysis to separate what contributes value from what does not. Coastal properties with bluff exposure or setback limits fall into this group as well. These factors are identified during the quote so any added fee is known up front.

Desktop or inspected?

A desktop report is completed without a site visit. The appraiser relies on public records, multiple listing service history, photographs and details supplied by the owner or attorney, then states those sources clearly in the report. It is well suited to estates, trusts, Proposition 19 filings and other situations where the home's condition is known or not disputed, and it can be prepared for property anywhere in California. An inspected report, either a Drive-By or a Standard, adds a physical viewing of the home. A Drive-By confirms the exterior, the street and the site from the road, while a Standard includes an interior walk-through with measurements and photographs. Inspected work is the better choice when condition is uncertain, when renovations or damage drive the value, or when the report is likely to face an opposing expert in court. The choice can be discussed before ordering.

Paying

Payment is collected upon delivery of the signed report rather than in advance. Cards, checks and bank transfers are accepted, and an itemized invoice is provided for estate accounting, trust records or tax files. Attorneys and fiduciaries who order on behalf of a client can have the invoice issued to the estate, the trust or the firm, whichever their records require.

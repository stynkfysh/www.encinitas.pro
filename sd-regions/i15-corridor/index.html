Interstate 15 Corridor

12 places in this part of San Diego County.

Heading north from Scripps Ranch and Mira Mesa, the Interstate 15 corridor passes Sabre Springs, Rancho Penasquitos, Rancho Bernardo and Carmel Mountain Ranch, with Poway on the east side and Torrey Highlands, Black Mountain Ranch, Del Sur and 4S Ranch on the west. Much of the housing was developed in phases from the 1970s through the 2000s, so homes from a single builder era tend to share floor plans, lot sizes and features, which supports close comparisons. Newer communities west of the freeway often carry Mello-Roos or other special assessments. Poway adds larger lots and semi-rural properties, and the San Pasqual Valley is mostly agricultural land. School district boundaries and homeowner association fees vary across short distances along the corridor.

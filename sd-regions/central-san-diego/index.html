Region

Central San Diego

21 places in this part of San Diego County.

Central San Diego includes downtown plus the older city neighborhoods surrounding Balboa Park and past it, namely the College Area, City Heights, Talmadge, Kensington, Normal Heights, University Heights, Golden Hill, South Park, North Park, Mission Hills, Hillcrest and Little Italy, as well as Skyline, Paradise Hills, Encanto, Logan Heights and Barrio Logan in the southeast. Housing ranges from early twentieth century Craftsman bungalows and Spanish Revival homes to downtown high-rise condominiums. Historic designations, Mills Act contracts and lot splits for accessory dwelling units are recurring factors in this area. Condominium valuations downtown hinge on building age, amenities and homeowner association finances, while bungalow values turn on original character, quality of restoration and how many units the lot can legally support.
